OUR STORY

What is Slash the Hate?

Social media has given people a powerful way to communicate, connect and share ideas.

But the same platforms can also be used to spread hate speech, cyberbullying, harassment and harmful content.

Slash the Hate was created to encourage people to pause before posting, understand the impact of their words and choose communication that respects others.

01 OUR MISSION

Educate. Prevent. Empower.

To educate social media users about harmful online behavior and empower them to communicate responsibly.

02 OUR VISION

A healthier digital world.

A digital environment where people can express themselves, disagree and connect without resorting to harassment, hate or abuse.
