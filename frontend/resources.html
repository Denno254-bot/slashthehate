DIGITAL SAFETY

Protect your digital space.

Small digital habits can make a significant difference to your online safety.

01

Protect your passwords

Use strong, unique passwords and avoid sharing them with other people.

02

Review your privacy settings

Understand who can see your posts, contact you and access information about you.

03

Think before sharing

Consider whether information is accurate, necessary and appropriate before sharing it.

04

Keep evidence of serious abuse

Where appropriate and safe, preserve relevant evidence before blocking or reporting harmful accounts.
