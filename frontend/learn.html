DIGITAL RESPECT • DIGITAL RESPONSIBILITY

Understand the HATE

The internet gives everyone a voice. Let's learn how to use that voice without becoming a source of harm.

KNOWLEDGE IS POWER

Before you fight hate, understand it

Harmful online behavior can take many forms. Sometimes people don't realize that their words, comments or actions can hurt others. Understanding these behaviors is the first step toward creating a healthier digital community.

01
⚠

Hate Speech

Communication that attacks, dehumanizes or promotes hostility toward people or groups based on characteristics such as identity or background.

02
💬

Cyberbullying

Repeated harmful behavior carried out through digital platforms that hurts, intimidates or humiliates another person.

03
🚫

Online Harassment

Repeated or targeted unwanted behavior online that can cause fear, distress, intimidation or disruption.

04
💬

Online Bodyshaming

Mocking, insulting or humiliating someone because of their physical appearance, body size or disability.

05
🤝

Digital Respect

Communicating online with consideration for other people's dignity, opinions, privacy and boundaries.

WHY IT MATTERS

Words can leave digital scars

A comment may take seconds to write, but its impact can remain with someone much longer.

Before sharing, commenting or replying, take a moment to consider the person on the other side of the screen.

01

Think

Pause before responding emotionally.

02

Verify

Check information before spreading it.

03

Respect

Disagree without attacking people.

04

Report

Don't amplify harmful content. Report it.

TAKE THE NEXT STEP

Your words have power

Use them to build conversations, not destroy people.
